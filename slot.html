Slot Machine

Moneten: 10

History

Rules
  • Each spin costs 1 money (the bet is always 1).
  • Numbers are 0 through 9.
  • Funny number: the three digits read together form a special number (like 69, 420, 67, 666, 42, 13, 314). Pays 50. (about a 0.7% chance)
  • Three of a kind: all three numbers match. Pays 40. (about a 0.9% chance)
  • Straight: three numbers in a row, ascending or descending (like 3-4-5 or 5-4-3). Pays 20. (about a 1.6% chance)
  • All odd / all even: all three numbers are odd, or all three are even (and not already a straight/triple/funny number). Pays 6. (about a 6% chance each)
  • Center shot: left and right numbers match. Pays out an amount equal to the center number. (about a 9% chance)
  • Double: any other two matching numbers. Pays 2. (about an 18% chance)
  • No match: you lose your bet. (about a 55% chance)
